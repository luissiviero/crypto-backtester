## Verdict
Do not close A1–A12 as "design choices the owner accepts at merge": A10 makes the exception the normal path for real Binance data and the Proof list never tests it; require an explicit, non-silent opt-in for intra-file holes and an acceptance test for A10 before the concern is closed.

## The case against
The easy answer is that A10 is the safe reading of the intent's "never a silently short frame". It is not safe; it is loud on every ordinary load. Binance publishes no candle for an interval with no trades (the public-data repository's issue tracker records it), so a 1m or 1s file for any symbol below the top tier has holes on normal days, and well-known outages (spot halted ~2 h on 2023-03-24; the 2021 futures halts) sit inside every long BTCUSDT range. Under A10 plus "a closed day is written whatever its source" (spec § Cache) such a day is cached with its hole, the REST fill never runs for it (REST is only for 404 days), and `load_klines` raises `KlineGapError` on every later load of that range, forever. The "one call returns a DataFrame" API the intent asked for (Ways to use it) becomes `try/except` boilerplate in every strategy, and `download` (A12) exits 1 on a prefetch of any such symbol, so a scripted warm-up loop fails on healthy data.
The intent betrayed: it enumerates the missing-data cases it cares about (pre-listing, delisted, unknown symbol) and offers two shapes, "raises a clear error or returns the list of gaps next to the data". The design took "raise" and then widened "gap" to intra-file holes the intent never named, with no way to say "I know" except catching the error; that is the result-object option re-introduced through `.frame`, minus the honesty of a parameter.
The proof hole: plan.md § Proof lists `test_day_missing_from_both_sources_raises_gap_error` and nothing for a candle missing inside a Vision file, nothing asserting `.frame` holds the found rows in schema, nothing on exact `Gap` boundaries despite Risks naming them the riskiest step. The weightiest assumption of the twelve would ship untested; a loader that silently fills the hole (forward-fill, or a grid off by one that hides it) passes every listed test.
Decide instead: keep raise as the default; add an explicit keyword (`allow_gaps: bool = False`, or equivalent) that returns the partial frame when the caller has said so, with the gaps reported on the CLI either way; distinguish "day absent from both sources" from "hole inside a source file" in `Gap.reason`; add to Proof a test with a fixture missing two consecutive candles that asserts the exact `Gap.start`/`end`/`candles`, the `.frame` rows, and that the opt-in returns the same rows. A11 and A12 are minor and can stay.
What would make me accept the obvious resolution: the owner stating that a backtest over an outage must fail loudly by design (then A10 stands) together with the A10 test in Proof; without the test, closing the concern accepts a behaviour nothing proves.
