## Verdict
Not a park: the design drops no REST behaviour that exists today and the intent never asks for resumability; record it as a plan risk / spec flagged concern for the owner, not a redesign before build.

## The case against
- The per-day durability was Vision's property, not REST's. Today's `fetch_range` (`app/data/rest.py:56-83`) catches nothing and `_rest_pass` (`loader.py:208-228`) settles days only after it returns, so a `DownloadError` from `with_retries` in the middle of a REST range throws that range's pages away today exactly as the design would. The design keeps today's REST shape; the thing it removes is the Vision pass that delivered the per-day write.
- The promise lives in the docstring of `sync_days`, which `app/data/__init__.py` does not export, and in `test_days_fetched_before_a_ban_stay_cached`, a Vision-plus-418 test. The ratchet names "418 ban" and the intent removes both Vision and the ban; the test falls with them, declared.
- The intent lists what callers use and what the cache keeps; a stopped run keeping its days is in neither. 0001 got it as a build choice from a review (0001 `plan.md:92-94`), never as a spec requirement, and 0001 intent's "each day is downloaded once" is about repeated loads of cached ranges, which R22 keeps. The item's fix adds a requirement the owner never wrote.
- The cost is time, not data. A `DownloadError` needs four consecutive failures of one page or a Retry-After over 60 s; the rerun refetches the range and KuCoin serves it again. At the measured 2000 per 30 s and cost 3, a year of futures 1m (about 2,600 pages) is about two minutes of calls. The rolling-window worry concerns cleared days, not days never cached. The owner's named runs are three days.
- The fix (settle during paging, or catch-and-re-raise around `fetch_range`) reshapes the loader in the area the plan calls riskiest (paging and completeness). Parking a design to force that in, for a robustness nicety, costs an iteration of the gate's two.
- The decision instead: continue, with a line in plan.md Risks (or a spec flagged concern): "a `DownloadError` mid-range discards that range's pages, as today's REST pass does; the rerun refetches them", so the owner can ask for the per-day settle in the build or a follow-up intent, with the test the item proposes.
- I would accept the park if the intent or 0001's accepted spec promised resumability to callers (they do not; only an internal docstring and a build note do), or if the futures rolling window were confirmed so that a lost rerun could lose days for good.
