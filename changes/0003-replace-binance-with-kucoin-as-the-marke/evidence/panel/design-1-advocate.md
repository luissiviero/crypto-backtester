## Verdict
Close the concern as the owner's decision, not the design's inference, and keep the note half of rule 4: the intent forbids the version bump, not the deprecation note.

## The case against
The easy answer is "pre-1.0 and one caller, so rule 4 does not apply; close it". That reasoning is nowhere in the policy: rule 4 (`framework/plugin/skills/data-conventions/SKILL.md`, no `policies/data-conventions.md` and no project override exist) says "additive changes only; a removed or renamed field is a new version with a deprecation note", with no exemption for pre-1.0 packages or for a single caller. Rule 4 has no deterministic backing (the skill: only the REVIEW.md pass at deploy re-checks it), so a concern closed on an inference the policy does not contain is exactly the kind the deploy pass reopens, after build and test are spent.
What the owner actually decided is in intent.md: "The version stays 0.1.0" (line 28), the three columns, `BinanceBanError`, `um` and `1s` "are removed" (lines 26, 31, 40, 46). That settles the *version* half of rule 4, by the owner's own words, and the panel should record it as such. It does not settle the *note* half: the intent nowhere says "no deprecation note", and the design and plan (Options not taken, "Bump the version or add a deprecation step") drop both together. The frame is the package's one contract (`app/data/schema.py` SCHEMA, eleven columns today), `__version__` stays 0.1.0 before and after, there is no changelog in the repository, and the only places the design promises to speak to callers (`load_klines` docstring, `download` epilog) mention futures volume and short frames but not the three columns that vanished. The next strategy the owner writes against 0.1.0 learns of `trades` only from a KeyError.
I would decide: close the concern with "the owner's intent keeps 0.1.0 and removes the fields; the design adds one line to the `load_klines` docstring and the `app/data/__init__.py` docstring naming the removed columns and `BinanceBanError`, with the reason (KuCoin does not publish them)". That is a sentence of copy, no version change, no code path, and it satisfies the half of rule 4 the intent left standing.
I would accept the obvious resolution if the concern's closing cites intent.md lines 26, 28 and 46 as the owner's decision over rule 4 (so the deploy pass finds a decision, not a guess) and the removed-column note lands in a docstring callers read; without the note the closing rests on an exemption the policy does not grant.
